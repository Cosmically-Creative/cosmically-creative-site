Web Hosting

Your website needs a reliable home on the internet. I use Nixihost for hosting, which comes with cPanel for easy file management. I'll handle the technical setup so you don't have to worry about servers, configurations, or uptime. Here's how it works:

1

Plan Selection

We'll go over your website's needs and pick the right Nixihost hosting plan for you. Whether it's a simple portfolio or a high-traffic business site, I'll make sure you have the right amount of storage, bandwidth, and resources without overpaying.

2

Setup & Configuration

I'll set up your hosting account, connect your domain, configure SSL certificates, and upload your website files through cPanel. Everything will be configured for security and performance right from the start.

3

Handoff & Training

Once everything is live, I'll walk you through cPanel so you know how to manage your files and handle basic tasks on your own. Or if you'd prefer, I can handle the whole thing for you. The choice is yours!
